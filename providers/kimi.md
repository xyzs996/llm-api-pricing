# Kimi costs and claims: every figure from a sentence that names it

4 of the 500 figures in these field notes come from a sentence that names Kimi. Each one is quoted verbatim from the write-up it appeared in, with the day that write-up went out.

Some of these are about Kimi and some only measure against it — a competitor's price quoted next to Kimi's belongs here too, because that is the sentence someone searching for the comparison is looking for. The sentence tells you which is which.

**This is not a Kimi price sheet.** Nothing here is read off a vendor page. These are figures a working developer wrote down, and the date is the day the piece was published — not the day the price was in force. Model prices move fast: read every row as of its own date, and treat an old one as a lead rather than a quote.
